SYSTEM PROTOCOL

HOW TO PLAY

Master cognitive response kinetics in 4 straightforward principles.

01

Select Mode & Start

Pick Easy, Medium, or Hard difficulty. Press Start Game or tap Spacebar to begin the 30-second run.

02

Spot The Active Core

One reactor panel will illuminate with a neon glow at a time. Zero in on the illuminated coordinate immediately.

03

Strike Instantly

Click, tap, or press the corresponding number key (1–9). Earn +10 base points. Each 5x streak activates score multipliers!

04

Beware Misses & Expiry

Clicking an inactive panel or letting an active target expire counts as a miss and breaks your streak multiplier.
